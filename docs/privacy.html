Privacy

Pickup is a free, non-commercial hobby project. There are no ads, nothing is for sale, and there is no business behind it.

Who is responsible

Felix Gnass · E-Mail

The app

Pickup runs on your Mac. When you share files, it shows a QR code. The phone that scans it loads a small web page directly from your Mac and receives the files directly from your Mac, over your local network.

  • No file, file name or other content is sent to me or to any server.
  • The app has no analytics, no crash reports and no update checks. It does not connect to the internet.
  • The QR code contains a one-time secret. Only a device that knows it gets access, and all file data is encrypted on the way.
  • For a preview of photos, videos and PDFs, the Mac makes small thumbnails with Quick Look. They are made on demand and are not kept.

This website

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
  • No accounts and no forms.
  • No fonts, scripts or other content from third-party servers. Links to other sites (for example GitHub) load nothing until you click them.
